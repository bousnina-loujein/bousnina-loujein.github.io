From the copper up

I'm an embedded and full-stack engineer based in Tunis. At RondeGuard I own the whole chain of a security patrol platform: the ESP32 terminal and its PCB, the secure MQTT link, the Node.js backend, the supervisor dashboard and the guards' mobile app.

I graduated from ESPRIT in 2026 with a National Engineering Degree in Ambient and Mobile Embedded Systems (SLEAM), after a bachelor's in electronics and industrial computing. I like problems where the hardware and the software have to be designed together.

Work

RondeGuard

In production, since February 2026

Security patrol traceability: guards scan NFC checkpoints on their rounds, and supervisors follow every round live. It began as my end-of-studies project at Maker Skills and became the product I now work on full time.

  • Custom hardware. ESP32 terminal with NFC checkpoint scanning and GSM connectivity, on a PCB I designed in Altium.
  • Secure by default. MQTT over TLS through EMQX Cloud, with credentials stored in ESP32 NVS.
  • Live supervision. React and TypeScript dashboard with Leaflet maps and Socket.IO tracking.
  • Built for the field. Flutter agent app with SOS, man-down detection from an MPU6050, push alerts and offline-first operation.
